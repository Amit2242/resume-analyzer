Associate Product Manager — Meesho (Bengaluru)

About the Role
Meesho is looking for an Associate Product Manager to join our consumer product team. 
You will work on defining features for millions of users across Bharat, running experiments, 
and collaborating with engineering and design to ship high-impact product improvements.

Requirements
- 0-2 years of experience in product management, business analysis, or a related role
- Strong analytical skills with proficiency in SQL — you should be comfortable querying data independently
- User empathy and a deep curiosity about customer behavior
- Familiarity with A/B testing and experimentation frameworks
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to work effectively with engineering, design, and business teams

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with product analytics tools such as Mixpanel, Amplitude, or Google Analytics
- Prior internship or project experience in product management
- Understanding of wireframing tools like Figma or Balsamiq
- Background in e-commerce or consumer internet

Tools You Will Use
- SQL, Excel / Google Sheets
- Mixpanel or Amplitude
- JIRA, Confluence
- Figma

Responsibilities
- Define product requirements and write clear PRDs and user stories
- Analyze product metrics and run A/B experiments to validate hypotheses
- Work closely with engineering, design, and data teams to ship product improvements
- Conduct user research and competitive analysis to inform product strategy
- Track and report on key product KPIs to stakeholders
- Contribute to the product roadmap and prioritization process

Why Join Meesho?
- Work on products used by 150M+ monthly active users
- Flat hierarchy with high ownership from day one
- Bengaluru office with flexible hybrid work policy
